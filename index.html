METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how Decision Unit intelligence becomes ABM value

In this Decision Unit System, value appears when account signals, stakeholder roles and relationship evidence become a structured, reusable input for Account-Based Marketing execution.

D
Data

Raw account data: organisations, contacts, roles, influence, pains, gains, objections, channels and relationship signals.

I
Information

The data is structured as a Decision Unit dataset: who participates, what each person needs, how they influence others and where the next action should happen.

I
Intelligence

The map interprets buying dynamics through authority, sponsorship, prescription, internal push, evidence gaps and account-readiness logic.

I
Insights

The system reveals consensus risks, missing stakeholders, message gaps, content needs and the relationship path required before a credible proposal can be made.

P
Personalization Actions

The output activates ABM journeys, contact-specific messages, content plays, CRM handover and final account orchestration.
